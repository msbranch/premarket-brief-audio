# The Tape Read — TikTok content

Apply these rules on **every** TikTok content request for The Tape Read.

## Product
Directional market call made **before the open**, grounded in options-flow data (put/call ratio, skew, volume), technical levels, and macro context. The call is graded after the close. **Hit rate: 64%.**

## Funnel
TikTok clip → Apple/Spotify podcast follow → daily brief listener → Ghost post link in episode description → free Monday brief → paid subscription.

## Clip selection
- **Never use the first 60 seconds.**
- Find the moment between **90 seconds and 4 minutes** where all three are true:
  1. a directional call is named (long / short / fade / hold),
  2. a specific data point is cited (put/call ratio, skew %, price level), and
  3. a reason is given.
- **Clip length: 45–60 seconds.**
- Structure: **hook → data point → call → open loop.**
- Never open with "Good morning," "Welcome back," or any setup sentence. **Start mid-thought.**

## Hook formulas
- **Bold call + number:** "Put/call hit 2.76 yesterday. That is a fear reading. I called the fade."
- **Outcome first:** "SPX dropped 1.8% by 10:30. That call was in the brief at 6am."
- **Contrarian:** "Everyone was buying the dip. The options market was screaming the opposite."
- **Specific level:** "4,820 was the line. The tape said it would hold. It didn't."
- **Stakes:** "If you missed what the put/call was saying Tuesday morning, here's what it cost."
- **Never use:** "In today's episode," "I want to talk about," "So basically," or rhetorical questions with no payoff.

## Caption formula
```
[Data point]. [Call made]. [Outcome]. Full brief + podcast: link in bio.
#optionstrading #premarket #[context-specific]
```
- First line **under 150 characters**.
- **No emoji. No generic phrases.**
- The outcome must be **real and verifiable from an actual brief**.

## Audiogram specs
- Vertical **9:16 (1080×1920px)**.
- Podcast cover art centered, animated waveform below, auto-captions at the bottom (white text on a dark strip).
- **No charts, no screen recordings, no talking head.**
- Tools: Headliner (auto-captions), getaudiogram.com (fastest).

## Bio link
- Points to the **most recent free Monday brief**, not the homepage. **Update every Monday.**
- `https://thetaperead.morganbranch.co/[current-monday-slug]/`

## Hashtags
- **Always:** `#optionstrading #premarket`
- **Rotate:** `#stockmarket #SPX #nasdaq #investing #daytrading #putcallratio`
- **Avoid:** `#finance #money #wealth #stocks`

## Posting
- **Monday–Friday, 6:30–8:00am ET.** One clip per brief.
- Retroactive posts: no date reference needed.

## Metrics (ranked)
1. Completion rate
2. Saves
3. Profile visits
4. Bio link clicks
5. Follower growth
6. Views

## Compliance

## Never post
- Fabricated data or hypothetical setups
- Motivational content or beginner explainers
- News-reaction content
- Anything not verifiable against an actual brief and scorecard
